Source: weather_daily_v (24h reads weather_10min_v, 7d/30d weather_hourly_v, and 24h+1 min weather_1min_v) — rollups of the Crodeon station (edi_crodeon_weather), pulled from the Crodeon API every 10 min by weather-sync.yml. Times are HST wall-clock. DLI 1d is the day's PAR integral (mol/m²/day); DLI 21d is the trailing 21-day average of it — same units, so both plot on one scale. Rain is a rate, so it is integrated over time rather than summed — a plain sum would scale with the sampling cadence. History before 05/07/26 is a 10-minute backfill; the live feed is 1-minute, and both integrate to the same figures. Buckets with under 90% sensor coverage are drawn as gaps, not as low values — the PAR sensor was absent until 11/26/24 and ramping through Dec 2024.
